Use the following RUBRIC to grade the summary. Each dimension is evaluated independently.

For dimensions citation, accurate, and thorough: apply the WORSE of (a) the quantitative % correctness and (b) the clinical severity threshold. That is, even if % correctness would yield a higher grade, a single severe error that can gravely influence clinical decision-making caps the score at 2.

<RUBRIC>

<citation>
    DESCRIPTION: Are citations present and appropriate? (Sind Zitate vorhanden und angemessen?)
    NOTE: Evaluate (1) whether citations exist and are correctly reproduced — exact wording and timing must match the original note — and (2) whether each citation is correctly attributed to the right category or assertion. Count each incorrectly represented citation as one error, regardless of how many mistakes it contains.
    EXCLUDE from this dimension: qualitative selection of which facts to cite.

    GRADES (apply the worse of % correctness and clinical severity):
    1 = 0–<25% of citations correct OR more than one severe error that can gravely influence clinical decision-making
    2 = 25–<50% of citations correct OR at least one severe error that can gravely influence clinical decision-making
    3 = 50–<75% of citations correct AND no severe error that can falsely influence clinical decisions
    4 = 75–<100% of citations correct AND no severe error that can falsely influence clinical decisions
    5 = 100% of citations correct AND no severe error that can falsely influence clinical decisions
</citation>

<accurate>
    DESCRIPTION: Is the summary accurate in extraction (extractive summarization)? (Ist die Zusammenfassung in der Extraktion genau?)
    NOTE: Incorrect information includes fabrication (entirely made-up content not present in the original) or falsification (distorted facts, e.g., "patient has Covid symptoms" rendered as "patient has Covid infection"). Each incorrect piece of information counts as a separate error.
    EXCLUDE from this dimension: omissions of relevant information (covered by thorough); citation timing or attribution errors (covered by citation).

    GRADES (apply the worse of % correctness and clinical severity):
    1 = 0–<25% of assertions correct OR more than one severe error that can gravely influence clinical decision-making
    2 = 25–<50% of assertions correct OR at least one severe error that can gravely influence clinical decision-making
    3 = 50–<75% of assertions correct AND no severe error that can falsely influence clinical decisions
    4 = 75–<100% of assertions correct AND no severe error that can falsely influence clinical decisions
    5 = 100% of assertions correct AND no severe error that can falsely influence clinical decisions
</accurate>

<thorough>
    DESCRIPTION: Is the summary thorough without any omissions? (Ist die Zusammenfassung vollständig ohne Auslassungen?)
    NOTE: All essential information from the original source must be present. This includes both patient-specific information and relevant contextual factors (e.g., environmental fall or delirium risk factors, not only patient-related ones). Each missing relevant piece of information counts as one error.
    EXCLUDE from this dimension: correctness of citation timing and attribution (covered by citation); correctness of extracted content (covered by accurate).

    GRADES (apply the worse of % completeness and clinical severity):
    1 = 0–<25% of essential information present OR more than one severe omission that can gravely influence clinical decision-making
    2 = 25–<50% of essential information present OR at least one severe omission that can gravely influence clinical decision-making
    3 = 50–<75% of essential information present AND no severe omission that can falsely influence clinical decisions
    4 = 75–<100% of essential information present AND no severe omission that can falsely influence clinical decisions
    5 = 100% of essential information present AND no severe omission that can falsely influence clinical decisions
</thorough>

<useful>
    DESCRIPTION: Is the summary useful? (Ist die Zusammenfassung nützlich?)
    NOTE: Assess whether the summary serves its intended purpose for the target audience — for nursing handover and surgical note use cases: all nursing-relevant information; for risk detection use cases (fall risk, delirium risk): presentation of relevant risk factors. Risk factors may be patient-related or environmental.
    EXCLUDE from this dimension: how information is presented — order (organized), wording (comprehensible), conciseness (succinct), synthesis level (abstraction).

    GRADES (degree of agreement with criterion):
    1 = 0–<25% of the summary is useful to the target audience
    2 = 25–<50% of the summary is useful to the target audience
    3 = 50–<75% of the summary is useful to the target audience
    4 = 75–99% of the summary is useful to the target audience
    5 = 100% of the summary is useful to the target audience
</useful>

<organized>
    DESCRIPTION: Is the summary organized? (Ist die Zusammenfassung gut strukturiert?)
    NOTE: Assess logical order of contents for nursing staff so that the patient's situation is easy to understand. Sections should be sensibly delineated and not confusing.
    EXCLUDE from this dimension: clarity of wording and language (comprehensible); conciseness (succinct); synthesis level (abstraction).

    GRADES (degree of agreement with criterion):
    1 = 0–<25% agreement with logical order and coherent structure
    2 = 25–<50% agreement with logical order and coherent structure
    3 = 50–<75% agreement with logical order and coherent structure
    4 = 75–99% agreement with logical order and coherent structure
    5 = 100% agreement — all assertions presented with logical order and coherent grouping
</organized>

<comprehensible>
    DESCRIPTION: Is the summary comprehensible with clarity of language? (Ist die Zusammenfassung verständlich und klar formuliert?)
    NOTE: Assess language clarity, absence of ambiguity, correct and appropriate use of technical terminology, and fluency. Language should be appropriate and familiar to the target user (nursing staff).
    EXCLUDE from this dimension: stigmatizing language (stig_original, stig_summary); redundancy and filler words (succinct).

    GRADES (degree of agreement with criterion):
    1 = 0–<25% agreement with clear, unambiguous, appropriately worded language
    2 = 25–<50% agreement with clear, unambiguous, appropriately worded language
    3 = 50–<75% agreement with clear, unambiguous, appropriately worded language
    4 = 75–99% agreement with clear, unambiguous, appropriately worded language
    5 = 100% agreement — language completely clear, fluent, and familiar to target user
</comprehensible>

<succinct>
    DESCRIPTION: Is the summary succinct with economy of language? (Ist die Zusammenfassung knapp und sprachlich ökonomisch?)
    NOTE: Assess absence of unnecessary repetitions and filler words. The summary should be to the point.
    EXCLUDE from this dimension: stigmatizing language (stig_original, stig_summary); overly complex or long sentences or wrong word choice (comprehensible); relevance of content (useful).

    GRADES (degree of agreement with criterion):
    1 = 0–<25% agreement — overly wordy with unnecessary repetitions and filler words throughout
    2 = 25–<50% agreement with economy of language
    3 = 50–<75% agreement with economy of language
    4 = 75–99% agreement with economy of language
    5 = 100% agreement — all assertions to the point, without any redundancy or filler words
</succinct>

<abstraction>
    DESCRIPTION: Is the summary abstracted and synthesized rather than a list of facts? (Ist die Zusammenfassung abstrahiert und synthetisiert, statt nur einer Auflistung von Fakten?)
    NOTE: Assess whether the summary interprets and synthesizes information rather than merely listing facts — e.g., through paraphrasing, drawing connections, or reasoning in the summary's own words.
    EXCLUDE from this dimension: correctness of interpretations (accurate, useful); economy of language (succinct); stigmatizing language (stig_original, stig_summary).

    GRADES (degree of agreement with criterion):
    1 = 0–<25% agreement — summary is a mere enumeration of facts without synthesis or interpretation
    2 = 25–<50% agreement with abstraction and synthesis
    3 = 50–<75% agreement with abstraction and synthesis
    4 = 75–99% agreement with abstraction and synthesis
    5 = 100% agreement — fully synthesized and interpreted summary in the model's own words
</abstraction>

<stig_original>
    DESCRIPTION: Is there presence of stigmatizing language in the original note? (Gibt es stigmatisierende Sprache in der Originalnotiz?)
    NOTE: Language must be unbiased and respectful toward the patient. Any single instance of stigmatizing language results in a score of 1.
    EXCLUDE from this dimension: correctness of clinical interpretation (accurate).

    GRADES:
    0 = No use of stigmatizing words
    1 = Definite use of stigmatizing words
</stig_original>

<stig_summary>
    DESCRIPTION: Is there presence of stigmatizing language in the summary? (Gibt es stigmatisierende Sprache in der Zusammenfassung?)
    NOTE: Language must be unbiased and respectful toward the patient. Any single instance of stigmatizing language results in a score of 1.
    EXCLUDE from this dimension: correctness of clinical interpretation (accurate).

    GRADES:
    0 = No use of stigmatizing words
    1 = Definite use of stigmatizing words
</stig_summary>

</RUBRIC>
